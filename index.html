Gnome Escape

Level 1 — The Overgrown Ruins

Stone Gnome — W jump, A/D move. Heavy, drowns in water.
Wind Gnome — ↑ jump (double), ←/→ move. Light, blown by gusts.

Get both gnomes onto the golden exit pad together. Stone Gnome can push the stone block onto the pressure plate to open the gate. Press R to reset the level.
